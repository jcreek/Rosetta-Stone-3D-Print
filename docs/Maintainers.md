The selected joint uses a 27.4 × 7.8 × 1.44 mm key with a 4.7 mm waist in a 1.56 mm pocket. Nominal clearance is 0.16 mm around the perimeter. The idealized geometry permits about 0.34 mm of seam opening before the shoulders catch. The 5.12 mm panels retain 2.00 mm of backing between the groove floor and key pockets.
