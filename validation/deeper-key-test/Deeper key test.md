# Deeper bow-tie fit comparison

Use the P1S with a 0.4 mm nozzle and the same PLA and print setup used for your first fit test. Map project filament 1 to black PLA and 2 to white PLA. The saved plate sliced without warnings at 39 min 11 sec and 7.43 g.

- **A, back row** uses 0.10 mm clearance around the key.
- **B, front row** uses 0.16 mm clearance around the key.
- The two keys are identical. Keep each pair of seam halves together. Mating halves are clipped from R2 C1 and R2 C2.

The key is 1.44 mm thick, double the previous 0.72 mm, and sits inside a 1.56 mm pocket. Panel thickness in this test is 5.12 mm with 2 mm of backing beneath the groove floors at the pocket. The groove depth remains 1.56 mm.

Remove loose bridge strands and any first-layer lip, close the seam on a flat surface, and press the key straight in from the back. Pick the tighter option that seats flush without needing force. With the key held seated, gently pull the panels apart and measure the open seam. Also check for front-to-back motion with the sample held against a flat board. The board captures the keys and carries the wall load.

Nominal key play in the geometric check is 0.21 mm for A and 0.34 mm for B. The earlier print showed that actual play can be larger than the modeled clearance. These are test dimensions. The existing twelve-panel project still uses the earlier pockets until the physical comparison identifies the better fit.
